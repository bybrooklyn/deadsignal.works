Dead Signal Works has a website now. I have been building the studio, Project
Meridian, and Meridian in places that made sense to me but were not especially
helpful to anyone trying to follow along. This is the public front door.

The useful parts are here: an overview of *Project Meridian*, a clear account of
what exists in the Meridian engine, documentation for people who want to
understand the architecture, and contact information that no longer requires
guessing an email address.

There are no game screenshots, trailers, or Steam page yet. Those will be added
when the work is ready and the links are real. Until then, the moving material
on this site is site artwork, not game footage.

Future updates will cover the game, the engine, and the occasionally strange
work of building both at once. This one gets to be about the site because the
site is what I finished today.
