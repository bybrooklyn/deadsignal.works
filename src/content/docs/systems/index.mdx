## Other architecture

Meridian’s adopted architecture also names Collective for optional online services, Isobar for weather and climate state, Basalt for terrain, Torsant for fluid simulation, and Marquee for a deferred post-1.0 promotional export workflow. Their names do not imply complete implementations.

## How systems meet

Ownership passes through typed contracts. Alluvium may author a recipe that produces terrain inputs, but Basalt owns terrain runtime behavior. Cairn owns physics queries, while gameplay decides what those queries mean. Penumbra renders snapshots without taking world authority. Wavefront owns audio behavior while Collective owns online session policy.
